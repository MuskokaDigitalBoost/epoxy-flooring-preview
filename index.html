Good questions, answered

Know what to ask before you coat.

The final site can expand these answers using the client’s verified systems, preparation method, timelines, and care guidance.

How do I choose the right coating system?

The right system depends on the existing concrete, moisture conditions, how the space is used, the desired finish, and maintenance expectations. Those details should be reviewed before a recommendation is made.

Can cracks and surface damage be repaired first?

Many surface issues can be addressed during preparation, but every slab is different. An assessment helps identify what can be repaired and what may continue to move over time.

How long will the space be out of use?

Installation and return-to-use timing varies with the coating system, site conditions, and project scope. A final schedule should be confirmed in the written proposal.

What do I need to do before installation?

Most projects require the floor and surrounding work area to be cleared. The installer should provide a project-specific checklist covering access, storage, temperature, and other responsibilities.
